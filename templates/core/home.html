Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about how we work, code ownership, project pricing, and our engineering standards.

What services does High Deckz offer?

We provide four core practices: AI and automation (workflow pipelines, chatbots, document parsing), custom software development (internal tools, APIs, web apps), web and mobile applications (responsive sites, Flutter and React Native apps), and IT consulting (infrastructure, system integration, technical advice). We also offer our ₹399 dynamic website for first-time clients.

What is included in the ₹399 first-time website offer?

The ₹399 offer provides a dynamic 3 to 4 page website engineered for your business with mobile responsiveness and transparent collaboration. Application hosting charges are applicable and billed separately. Domain, extra pages, and custom feature requests are quoted separately.

Do you work on custom projects or only standard packages?

Yes. Custom solutions are central to what we do. We work with you to understand your requirements and build software, applications, and automation tailored specifically to your operational needs.

How much does a custom project cost?

Most projects fit within ₹1,00,000. Project costs depend on scope, complexity, timeline, and specific technical requirements. After an initial requirements discussion, we provide a clear proposal with defined deliverables and transparent milestone-based pricing.

Who owns the source code and deliverables?

You do. You receive 100% full ownership of the custom code, architecture assets, and deliverables developed for your project. There is zero proprietary platform lock-in.

Can you integrate with our existing systems?

In most cases, yes. We evaluate your existing tools, databases, and third-party software to determine integration feasibility through REST APIs, webhooks, and secure database connections.
